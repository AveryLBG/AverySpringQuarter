1. A business can grow without tracking anything.
No, data is needed to make good decisions.
2. Follower count is the best way to measure success.
Absolutely not. There are many "businesses" with many followers and barely any sales.
3. Customer opinions matter more than sales numbers.
Not in terms of business. If opinions are bad but you're still making money, you're still making money.
4. A good decision can be made with no data.
No. At that point it'd just be up to luck, and the odds of the decision being good are extremely low.
5. Data is always fair.
No, data is skewed extremely easily. Even the act of collecting data can skew it if done by the wrong methods.
